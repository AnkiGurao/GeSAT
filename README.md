GeSAT is developed using R (v4.2 or higher) and the Shiny framework, hosted on shinyapps.io (https://rsklabindia.shinyapps.io/GeSAT/)

Welcome to GeSAT — the Gene Stability Analysis Tool.

GeSAT, shiny interface, helps to determine most stably expressed genes among the panel of internal control genes.

The workflow is simple and structured:

Step one — Data Upload,

Step two — Standard curve analysis and PCR efficiency calculation,

Step three — Ct Distribution and Quality Control,

Step four — BestKeeper,

Step five — geNorm,

Step six — Mixed Model Stability,

Step seven — Delta Ct method,

Step seven — NormFinder,

Step eight — the Cumulative Ranking (using the various ranking parameters) and summary report.

Why Choose GeSAT?
GeSAT empowers researchers to identify the most stable reference genes for qPCR data normalization using advanced, peer-reviewed methods. Upload your gene expression data and get a comprehensive stability ranking in minutes.

✅ Comprehensive Analysis
Utilizes multiple established methods including Delta Ct, NormFinder, geNorm, BestKeeper, and Mixed Model for robust results.

✅ User-Friendly Interface
Simply upload your data and explore intuitive visualizations of gene stability rankings.

✅ Accurate and Reliable
Combines results from multiple algorithms to provide a cumulative ranking, ensuring dependable reference gene selection.
